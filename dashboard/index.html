Three findings

  1. The Christmas run-up drives the year.

    September to November 2011 brought 38% of the revenue in the 12 full months, and November (£1.13M) was 2.1× the January–August monthly average. Plan stock and staff from August.

  2. A small group of customers carries the business.

    The top 20% of customers brought 74% of revenue; the top 1% (about 43 customers) brought 30%. 35% of customers ordered only once. Look after the big accounts and give one-time buyers a reason to return.

  3. Sales are mostly British, with clear next markets.

    The UK brought 82% of revenue. The Netherlands, Ireland, Germany and France together brought 11%, so test growth there first.

How the data was cleaned

A cancellation is matched to the purchase it undid (same customer, item and quantity), and both are removed. Without this, one 80,995-unit order that was cancelled 12 minutes later would count as £168k of sales.
